# AirType – hand keyboard extension

Type on any web page by pointing and pinching in the air. Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave.

## Install (2 minutes)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (don't delete it later).
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and switch on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped `airtype-ext` folder.
4. A setup tab opens. Press **Allow** for the camera (one time only).
5. Pin the AirType icon (puzzle-piece menu > pin).

## Use
- On any page click the AirType icon, or press **Alt+Shift+K**.
- Click a text box first (Google's search box is focused automatically if nothing is).
- Move your index finger over a key and **pinch thumb + index** to press it.
- Drag the top bar to move the panel. ⇔ changes size. ✕ closes it and turns the camera off.
- Click the icon again to hide it.

## Typing in the dark
- 🌙 **Auto** switches on a bright "fill-light" panel when the camera sees a dark room, so your screen lights your hand. 🌙 On / Off forces it.
- Brightness is auto-boosted, and the slider adds more.
- In very dark rooms the camera drops to ~15 fps to get longer exposure (brighter picture, slightly slower tracking).
- Tips: turn your screen brightness up, keep your hand about 30–50 cm from the camera, and use Dwell mode (hold a fingertip on a key) if pinching is unreliable.
- A normal webcam sees visible light only. It cannot see in total darkness, and this extension cannot use an infrared (Windows Hello) camera.

## Notes
- Everything runs on your computer. The hand model is bundled, with no internet needed and no video uploaded.
- Doesn't work on chrome:// pages, the Chrome Web Store or the built-in PDF viewer (the icon shows an X).
